Play statistics & privacy

We measure how the game performs and which challenges people play, so we can improve it.

What the game sends

While playing, the game reports foreground playtime, completed challenge numbers, solo or multiplayer mode, and whether the build is from our website or itch.io. Shared links may include a small set of approved campaign labels, such as “reddit” or “launch”. We discard all other campaign text.

A new random token is held in memory for each page load, solely to avoid counting repeated reports twice. It is never saved in a cookie or browser storage. Reloading or opening another tab starts a new session. We do not recognise returning visitors or connect visits across sites.

What is kept

Our relay saves aggregate counts and statistical summaries, not individual session histories or tokens. We do not save IP addresses, IP hashes, fingerprints, locations, account information, full URLs or referrers in these statistics. Aggregates are retained for 30 days plus the current day.

The server also records operational totals such as connected players, occupied rooms, CPU and network usage. Source addresses are handled temporarily to deliver traffic and limit abuse, but new request logs on our relay are disabled. Hosting services, including GitHub Pages and itch.io, have their own privacy policies and technical processing.

Your choice

The game honours Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track by disabling client play-statistics requests. You can also play with these statistics off for this visit. Server operational totals still describe the service as a whole.

Gameplay preferences such as sound and speed units may be saved locally to remember your choices; they are not used for analytics.
